05 · Behind the scenes

How this website was made.

Zielona Pergola is a showcase website built with AI — from the wireframe, through the copy, to a complete set of photorealistic images generated precisely for the project's frames. Drag the slider to see the difference between a dimensional placeholder and the final image.

  1. Wireframe and copy — page structure, sections and microcopy refined iteratively with AI.
  2. Dimensional placeholders — 36 frames in their target proportions, before a single image existed.
  3. Photorealistic AI imagery — a full set of photos with a consistent feel of light, greenery and gold.
  4. Living details and automations — subtle image animations, a background video in the hero and a form wired to an automation (n8n) that stores enquiries and sends them by email.
