Before you call

To prepare a proposal, we need a few details.

You don't need to have everything worked out — the more we know, the sooner we come back with a concrete proposal.

  • Type of event — wedding, First Communion, conference, company meeting…
  • Planned date — exact or approximate.
  • Location — where the event takes place.
  • Number of guests — even a rough estimate.
  • Type of meals — buffet, plated dishes, coffee breaks…
  • Waiting staff — whether you'll need them.
  • Special requirements — diets, allergies, particular requests.
