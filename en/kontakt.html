Reservations

Book a table.

Pick a date and we'll take care of the rest — your booking goes straight into our calendar, and we'll send you a confirmation by e-mail. For groups of more than 12, please give us a call.

Pick a table on the floor plan (optional) — we will note it in your reservation.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Where is the Restaurant located and what are the opening hours?
Zielona Pergola Restaurant: ul. Cyprysowa 12, 00-000 Zielony Gaj (catering: ul. Cyprysowa 14, right next door). Open daily: Mon–Fri 8:00–21:00, Sat 11:00–22:00, Sun 11:00–20:00.
Is there parking at the restaurant?
Yes — free guest parking (approx. 20 spaces) right next to the restaurant, entered from Cyprysowa Street. The main entrance is on the garden side, step-free.
Can I book a table for a larger group?
Online reservations cover up to 12 guests. Larger groups and events are handled as group bookings — call 795 870 359 or use the catering enquiry form.
Is the venue accessible for guests with limited mobility?
Yes — a step-free entrance on the garden side, a spacious dining room and an accessible restroom. We’ll gladly help you pick a comfortable table.
Can I bring my pet?
Pets are welcome in the garden area and on the terrace — we just ask for a leash and a little consideration for other guests.
